node-tikzjax Demo

Render TikZ diagrams directly to SVG in Node.js with WebAssembly TeX and full Unicode support.

Unicode Character Sets (New Support)

1. Chinese (中文)

Simplified / Traditional Chinese & CJK punctuation

2. Japanese (日本語)

Hiragana, Katakana & Kanji (漢字・ひらがな・カタカナ)

3. Korean (한국어)

Hangul syllables and data flow diagrams

4. Cyrillic (Русский)

Russian alphabet in state machines / automata

5. Greek & Math Symbols

α, β, γ, θ, λ, π, Ω, ≤, ≥, ≠, ≈, ±, ∑, ∫, ∂, etc.

6. European Latin Accents

Français (é, è, ô), Español (¡, ñ, á), Deutsch (ü, ö, ß), Nordic (ø, å)

7. Emojis & Symbols

Modern symbols (💻, ⚡, 🔒, ☁, 🚀, ☕, ★, 😊)
